In Loving Memory of Eleanor Vance
March 14, 1948 — November 22, 2025 · Service: St. Luke's Chapel

A devoted teacher, gardener, and beloved matriarch whose warmth and storytelling touched generations of students and family members.

Words from Spouse & Children
“What's something they did every day that you didn't appreciate enough until now?”
Every morning at 6:30, she'd leave a handwritten note on the kitchen counter with an inspirational thought or a reminder of something to look forward to.
— Robert Vance (Spouse)
“What's a moment she made you proud in a way that surprised you?”
When she organized the community library drive at age 72, collecting over 3,000 books for underprivileged schools. She never looked for recognition.
— Claire Vance (Daughter)
Memories from Friends & Colleagues
“What did she teach you about being a friend?”
She taught me that showing up with soup and listening without trying to fix everything is the highest form of companionship.
— Martha Hayes (Friend of 40 years)
